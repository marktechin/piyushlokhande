How to Hire a Facebook Ads Expert: 10 Questions to Ask

By Piyush Lokhande, Meta & Google Ads expert · 15 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer

Before hiring a Facebook or Meta Ads expert, check their real results in your type of business, how they track leads, who owns the ad account, how they report, and how they test creatives. A good expert talks about cost per lead and cost per customer, not likes and reach.

Why hiring the right person matters

A good Meta Ads expert can turn a small budget into a steady flow of leads. A bad one can spend your money on likes, poor-quality leads and boosted posts. These 10 questions will help you tell the difference, whether you're hiring a freelancer, an agency or a specialist abroad.

The 10 questions

  1. Have you worked with businesses like mine? Ask for examples in your industry or a similar one, and what the main result was.
  2. What will you measure? The answer should be leads, cost per lead, sales or ROAS, not likes, reach or followers.
  3. How will you track results? Look for Meta Pixel, Conversions API and a clear way to know which ads brought which leads.
  4. Who owns the ad account? It should be your Business Manager and ad account. You should never lose your data if you stop working together.
  5. Will you boost posts or use Ads Manager? Real campaigns are built in Ads Manager with proper objectives, not with the Boost button.
  6. How do you test creatives? A good expert tests multiple hooks, formats and offers and explains what won and why.
  7. What do you need from me? Clear expectations about creatives, landing pages, offers and response times show experience.
  8. How often will I get reports? Weekly or monthly reports with numbers and next steps are a good sign.
  9. What budget do you recommend and why? A good answer is based on your goals, city and customer value, not a random number.
  10. What happens in the first 30 days? Look for a clear plan: setup, tracking, testing, then optimisation.

Red flags to watch out for

  • Guaranteed numbers of leads or sales before seeing your business
  • Running ads from their own account instead of yours
  • No interest in your offer, pricing or follow-up process
  • Reports full of reach and impressions, without leads or sales
  • Pressure to sign long contracts before any results

Freelancer, agency or specialist abroad?

Agencies offer more people but you may be handled by a junior. Freelancers give you direct access to the person running ads, but experience varies a lot. Many businesses in the US, UK, Australia and the Middle East now hire experienced specialists in India, who offer senior-level work at a more reasonable cost, as long as communication and time-zone overlap are clear.

Tip: Whoever you hire, keep your ad account, pixel and data in your own name, and ask for a short paid trial or a clear first-month plan before committing long term.

FAQs

How much does a Facebook Ads expert charge?

+

It varies widely by experience, country and scope. Some charge a fixed monthly fee, others a percentage of ad spend. Compare based on expected results and what's included, not only on price.

Should I hire a freelancer or an agency for Facebook Ads?

+

A freelancer gives you direct access to the person running your ads. An agency gives you a bigger team. For small and mid-size businesses, an experienced specialist is often the better value.

Can I hire a Meta Ads expert from India for a US or UK business?

+

Yes. Meta Ads is managed online, and many businesses abroad work with specialists in India. Make sure campaigns run in your own ad account and agree on communication times.
