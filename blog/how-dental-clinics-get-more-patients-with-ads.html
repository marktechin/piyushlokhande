How Dental Clinics Can Get More Patients with Online Ads

By Piyush Lokhande, Meta & Google Ads expert · 15 Nov 2026 · 8 min read

Quick answer

Dental clinics get the most patients online by combining four things: a complete Google Business Profile with reviews, Google Search ads for treatment keywords, location-based Meta Ads on Facebook and Instagram, and fast WhatsApp or phone follow-up on every enquiry.

Why online ads work for dental clinics

Most patients now find a dentist by searching on Google or seeing a clinic on Instagram, then checking reviews before they call. A clinic that shows up in those moments, with good reviews and an easy way to book, wins the appointment. The good news is that dentistry is local, so you only need to win patients within a few kilometres of your clinic.

Step 1: Fix your Google Business Profile first

Before spending on ads, make sure your free Google Business Profile is complete. It's what appears on Google Maps when someone searches "dentist near me".

  • Correct clinic name, address, phone and working hours
  • Real photos of the clinic, team and equipment
  • All treatments listed as services
  • A steady flow of genuine Google reviews from patients
  • Regular posts about treatments, offers and patient education
Tip: Ask every happy patient for a review before they leave. A QR code at the reception or a WhatsApp message with your review link makes this easy.

Step 2: Run Google Search ads for treatment keywords

Google Ads lets you appear at the top when people search for specific treatments. Patients searching "root canal treatment near me" or "dental implant cost" are already looking for help.

  • Create separate ad groups for each treatment: implants, root canal, braces and aligners, cleaning, children's dentistry
  • Target only the area your patients realistically come from
  • Use call assets so patients can call with one tap
  • Add negative keywords like "free", "course", "job" and "college" to avoid wasted clicks
  • Track calls and form leads as conversions

Step 3: Use Meta Ads to reach people nearby

Facebook and Instagram ads work well for treatments people delay or don't search for actively, like teeth whitening, aligners, smile makeovers and family check-ups. Target people within a few kilometres of the clinic and use videos of the doctor explaining a treatment, clinic walk-throughs and patient education.

Click-to-WhatsApp ads are especially effective in India because patients can ask about timing and cost before committing.

Step 4: Follow up fast

The biggest leak in clinic marketing is slow follow-up. A patient who enquires and doesn't hear back within an hour has often already booked somewhere else. Make one person responsible for answering calls and WhatsApp enquiries from ads, and track how many enquiries turn into appointments.

Step 5: Stay within advertising rules

Healthcare ads must follow Meta and Google policies and professional guidelines. Avoid guaranteed results, misleading before-and-after claims and targeting based on health conditions. Clear, honest information about treatments builds more trust anyway.

What results to expect

Results depend on your city, competition, treatments and how quickly enquiries are followed up. Most clinics see enquiries start within the first weeks, with costs improving after an initial testing period of 2 to 4 weeks. Track three numbers every month: enquiries, appointments booked, and cost per appointment.

FAQs

Are Google Ads allowed for dental clinics?

+

Yes. Dental clinics can advertise on Google and Meta as long as ads follow healthcare advertising policies, with no misleading claims or guaranteed outcomes.

Which treatments are best to advertise?

+

Higher-value treatments like implants, aligners, braces and root canals usually give the best return, along with general check-ups to bring in new families.

How long before a clinic gets patients from ads?

+

Enquiries usually start within the first weeks. The first 2 to 4 weeks are a testing phase, and costs typically improve after that.
