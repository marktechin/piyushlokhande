Meta Ads vs Google Ads: Which Is Better for Your Business?

By Piyush Lokhande, Meta & Google Ads expert · 15 Nov 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Use Google Ads when people already search for what you sell, and Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) when you need to create demand or sell visually. Most local businesses get the lowest cost per customer by using Google Ads to capture ready buyers and Meta Ads to reach new people nearby and retarget visitors.

The short answer

Google Ads captures demand. Meta Ads creates demand. If people already type your service into Google, like "dentist near me" or "CBSE school in Indore", Google Ads puts you in front of them at the moment they're ready to act. If people don't search for what you sell, or you sell something visual like clothing, jewellery or interiors, Meta Ads on Facebook and Instagram lets you reach the right people before they start searching.

How Google Ads works

Google Ads shows your ad when someone searches for a keyword you've chosen. You pay when someone clicks. Because the person is actively looking, these leads tend to be high intent and convert quickly.

  • Best for: services people search for (doctors, schools, repairs, B2B suppliers, legal, real estate)
  • Strengths: high intent, measurable calls and enquiries, good for urgent needs
  • Weaknesses: limited by search volume, clicks can be expensive in competitive categories

How Meta Ads works

Meta Ads shows your ad to people on Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp based on location, age, interests and behaviour. People aren't searching, so your creative has to stop the scroll and make them interested.

  • Best for: fashion, retail, D2C brands, clinics' cosmetic treatments, schools' admission awareness, events, restaurants
  • Strengths: cheaper reach, great for visual products, strong for WhatsApp and lead form campaigns
  • Weaknesses: lower intent, lead quality depends heavily on the offer and the form

Which should you choose? Examples by business type

  • Dental clinic or hospital: start with Google Search ads for treatments, add Meta Ads for nearby awareness and cosmetic treatments.
  • School or coaching institute: Meta Ads to reach parents in your area before admissions, Google Ads for parents searching during admission season.
  • Clothing or fashion brand: Meta Ads first, especially Reels and catalogue ads. Google Shopping later if you sell online.
  • B2B manufacturer or supplier: Google Search for buyers looking for suppliers, plus LinkedIn or Meta lead forms for retailers and decision-makers.
  • Local services (interiors, repairs, real estate): Google Ads for ready buyers, Meta Ads with strong before-and-after creatives.

Why most businesses should use both

The two platforms work best together. Someone sees your Reel on Instagram, forgets about it, and a week later searches your name or service on Google. Without Google Ads you may lose them to a competitor. Without Meta Ads they may never have known you existed. Retargeting also works across both: people who visited your site can see a reminder on Instagram.

Tip: Start with one platform where your customers clearly are, get the cost per lead stable, then add the second platform. Splitting a small budget across too many campaigns is one of the most common reasons ads "don't work".

Common mistakes on both platforms

  1. No conversion tracking, so you can't tell which ads bring leads
  2. Boosting posts instead of running proper campaigns in Ads Manager
  3. Broad keywords on Google without negative keywords
  4. Sending ad traffic to a slow website with no clear call button
  5. Not calling leads back quickly, often the biggest leak of all

FAQs

Is Google Ads more expensive than Facebook Ads?

+

Cost per click is usually higher on Google, but leads are often more ready to buy. What matters is cost per customer, not cost per click, and that varies by industry.

Can a small business run both Google Ads and Meta Ads?

+

Yes, but start with one platform, get stable results, then add the other. A small budget split across too many campaigns rarely works.

Which is better for local businesses in India?

+

For services people search for, Google Ads. For visual products and creating awareness in a local area, Meta Ads. Many local businesses in cities like Indore get the best results using both.
