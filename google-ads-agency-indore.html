Why Google Ads works for local businesses

Google Ads is different from social media ads. On Instagram you interrupt people while they scroll. On Google, people come to you with a problem they want solved right now. That's why Google Ads leads are often higher intent and convert faster.

The catch is that Google Ads can burn money fast if it's set up badly. Broad keywords, no negative keywords and no conversion tracking are the most common reasons businesses say "Google Ads doesn't work for us".

I build campaigns around the exact services you offer and the areas you serve, track every call and enquiry, and cut spend on searches that don't bring business. Combined with a fast, clear landing page and a strong Google Business Profile, this is one of the most reliable ways to get steady enquiries.

Frequently asked questions

Is Google Ads better than Facebook Ads?

+

Neither is better for everyone. Google Ads captures people already searching for your service, so leads are high intent. Facebook and Instagram ads create demand and are cheaper per impression. Many businesses get the best results using both together.

How much budget do I need for Google Ads in Indore?

+

It depends on how competitive your keywords are. Some local services have cheap clicks, while others like dental implants or real estate cost more. I'll check real keyword costs for your service and suggest a starting budget on a free call.

Can I get phone calls directly from Google Ads?

+

Yes. Call assets and call-only ads let people call you with one tap from the search results, and calls can be tracked as conversions.

Do you also help with Google Maps and Google Business Profile?

+

Yes. A complete Google Business Profile with reviews makes local ads perform better, and I'll guide you on setting it up properly.

Why am I getting clicks but no leads from Google Ads?

+

Usually it's broad keywords, missing negative keywords, no conversion tracking or a weak landing page. A Google Ads audit will show exactly where the money is leaking.
