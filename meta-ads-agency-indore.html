How I run Meta Ads that actually convert

Most Facebook ads fail for one of three reasons: the wrong objective, a weak offer, or no follow-up after the lead comes in. Boosting a post from your page usually brings likes and comments from people who will never buy.

I start with your offer and your customer. A dental clinic in Indore, a school running admissions and a clothing store in Delhi need completely different campaigns. Then I build a simple funnel: an ad that grabs attention, a form or WhatsApp chat that filters out time-wasters, and retargeting for people who showed interest but didn't contact you.

Once the campaign is live, I watch cost per lead and lead quality every day. Creatives and audiences that work get more budget, and the rest are switched off. That's how ₹50L+ of ad spend has been managed across clinics, schools, fashion brands and B2B companies.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to run Facebook ads in Indore?

+

There are two costs: your ad budget, which is paid directly to Meta, and the management fee. Ad budget depends on your goal and competition. Many local businesses start with a small monthly test budget and increase it once the cost per lead is clear. I'll suggest a realistic number on a free call.

Is boosting a post the same as running Meta Ads?

+

No. Boosting is a simplified option with limited targeting and goals. A proper Meta Ads campaign uses Ads Manager with the right objective, tested audiences, lead forms or WhatsApp, and tracking, which usually means far better leads for the same money.

How long before Facebook ads start working?

+

Ads can bring enquiries within the first few days. The first 2 to 4 weeks are a learning and testing phase, and cost per lead usually drops and stabilises after that.

Do you run Instagram ads as well?

+

Yes. Meta Ads covers both Facebook and Instagram, including Reels, Stories and Feed placements. Placements are chosen based on where your audience responds best.

Can you get leads directly on WhatsApp?

+

Yes. Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a chat with your business directly, which works very well for clinics, retail stores and local services in India.
