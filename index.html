Which is better for my business, Meta Ads or Google Ads?

+

Google Ads works best when people already search for your service, like "dentist near me" or "school admission in Indore". Meta Ads works best for creating demand and reaching people by interest, age and location. Most local businesses get the best results using both, and I'll recommend the right split after looking at your business.

How much budget do I need to start running ads?

+

It depends on your city, competition and goal. Many local businesses start with a modest monthly test budget and increase it once cost per lead is clear. On the free call I'll suggest a realistic starting budget for your case.

How soon will I start getting leads?

+

Campaigns usually start getting enquiries within the first days after launch. The first 2 to 4 weeks are for testing audiences, creatives and offers, and results become more stable and cheaper after that.

Do you create the ad creatives and Reels too?

+

Yes. I plan the ad creatives, hooks and Reels along with the campaigns, so the message, the creative and the landing page all work together.

How will I know if my ads are working?

+

I set up tracking for calls, WhatsApp clicks and form leads, and share regular reports on leads, cost per lead and what's being improved, so you always know what your money is doing.
