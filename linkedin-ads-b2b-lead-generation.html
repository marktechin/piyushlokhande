How B2B lead generation is different

In B2B, one customer can be worth lakhs of rupees, and the buying decision often involves more than one person. That means fewer but higher-value leads, longer sales cycles and much more focus on lead quality than lead volume.

LinkedIn lets you target by job title and company, which no other platform does as precisely. For many Indian B2B brands, a mix works best: LinkedIn for decision-makers, Google Search for buyers already looking for a supplier, and Meta Ads for retailers and small business owners who spend time on Instagram and Facebook.

I've used this approach for a B2B clothing brand reaching retailers and bulk buyers, a Pune company selling interior solutions to schools and offices, and a New York agency building its client pipeline.

Frequently asked questions

Are LinkedIn Ads worth it for Indian B2B companies?

+

For high-value products and services, yes. LinkedIn clicks cost more than Meta or Google, but the precise job-title targeting can make the cost per qualified lead lower when one customer is worth a lot.

Can Meta Ads work for B2B?

+

Yes, especially when your buyers are small business owners, shopkeepers or retailers who use Instagram and Facebook daily. Lead forms with qualifying questions help filter for serious buyers.

How do you make sure B2B leads are good quality?

+

By targeting the right roles, using forms that ask about business type, requirement and quantity, and optimising campaigns for qualified leads rather than the cheapest leads.

Do you work with B2B companies outside India?

+

Yes. I've worked with a New York-based agency and can run campaigns for US and international markets.
