How fashion brands grow with Meta Ads

In fashion, the creative is the campaign. The same budget can bring ten times more sales with the right video, model, styling and hook. That's why I plan creatives and ads together instead of just boosting product photos.

Most fashion brands also have more than one goal: online orders, WhatsApp enquiries, store visits and sometimes wholesale. Each needs a different campaign and audience, and mixing them in one ad usually wastes money.

I build separate campaigns for each goal, use retargeting to bring back people who viewed products, and push budget hard during festive and wedding seasons when buying intent is highest. This helped Shlay Clothing in Delhi grow both online sales and in-store footfall, and NKD Group reach retailers and bulk buyers.

Frequently asked questions

Do Instagram ads work for small clothing stores?

+

Yes. Location-based Instagram and Facebook ads with good Reels can bring both online enquiries and store visits, even on a modest budget.

Should I sell on WhatsApp or on a website?

+

Both can work. WhatsApp is great for boutiques and personalised selling. A website with a catalogue works better for scaling online orders. Ads can be set up for either.

When should fashion brands spend the most on ads?

+

During peak buying seasons like Diwali, wedding season and end-of-season sales. Planning creatives and budgets a few weeks in advance makes a big difference.

Can you help with wholesale clothing leads?

+

Yes. Wholesale campaigns target retailers and boutique owners with lead forms that ask about their business and order size.
