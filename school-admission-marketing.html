How schools get more admissions online

Parents compare several schools before deciding. They look at fees, distance, board, facilities, results and what other parents say. Your ads need to answer these questions clearly and make it easy to book a campus visit.

I combine two approaches. Google Ads catches parents who are actively searching for schools in your area. Meta Ads on Facebook and Instagram reaches parents nearby who may not have started searching yet, using campus videos and admission announcements.

Speed matters: an enquiry called back within an hour is far more likely to visit than one called the next day. That's why every campaign comes with a simple follow-up process. This approach helped Sonali Public School in Guna generate quality admission enquiries.

Frequently asked questions

When should a school start admission advertising?

+

Ideally 4 to 8 weeks before admissions open, so your school is on the parents' shortlist early. Campaigns then continue through the admission window.

Do Facebook ads work for school admissions?

+

Yes. Most parents use Facebook and Instagram daily, and location-based campaigns with campus videos work very well for generating admission enquiries.

Can you target parents only near our school?

+

Yes. Campaigns can be limited to a radius around your campus or specific areas of the city, so you only pay to reach families who can realistically attend.

Do you work with coaching institutes too?

+

Yes. The same approach works for coaching institutes, with campaigns around batch start dates, demo classes and results.
